Nice to meet you, I'm Eric!

I am a Biomedical Engineering graduate from NUS with highest distinction, specializing in neural systems, gait analysis, and brain-computer interfaces. My research spans non-human primate spatial memory, EEG/MEG signal processing, and wearable IMU-based motion sensing, and working on a publication in trajectory estimation. Alongside academic work, I've contributed to projects at EPFL TNE Lab, NUS iHealthtech, and NUS N.1 Lab and blending engineering in my daily projects.
