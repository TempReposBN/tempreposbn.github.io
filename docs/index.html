Research Projects
Project 1

Hippocampal Theta During Goal-Directed Navigation

We analyze hippocampal theta-band dynamics in macaque monkeys performing a goal-directed virtual navigation task. Focusing on the pre-navigation (path-planning) phase, we show how theta power and peak amplitude encode goal cues and spatial context, revealing the hippocampus's dual role in recognizing and binding “what” and “where” information for memory-guided navigation.

Theta (3 - 8 Hz) Signal Processing Spatial Memory LFP Analysis Virtual Navigation
EEG/LFP • Python • Unity Engine

A zero-velocity-aided pipeline with RTS-Kalman smoothing and soft stance detection to curb drift during long indoor walks. Benchmarked with Bland–Altman analysis.
